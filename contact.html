Habits questions we can help with

Streaks and progress: how a streak is counted, why a streak has not broken yet in the morning, how today's progress percentage is calculated, and what the daily score in History means.

Managing habits: adding your own habits, removing one of the starter habits — Namaz, Recite Holy Quran, Exercise, Learning AI and Video Idea — and what happens to past history when a habit is deleted.

History and missing days: how to review a past date, why a day looks empty, and how far back the history panel goes. Because your record is stored only on your device, we cannot recover a cleared history — but we can explain exactly what happened and how to protect it next time, for example by keeping the app installed as a PWA and not clearing site data for it.

Reporting a bug

Please include your device, your browser, and the steps: what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead. Mention the date shown at the top of the app if a streak or history problem is involved, because dates are how the app organises everything. Never send personal routine details you would not want in an email — a short description is always enough.

Ideas for new habit features are welcome as well.
