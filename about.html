About Loona Habits App

Loona Habits App · Loona Automation

Small habits daily, big success honestly

Loona Habits App is a simple, free daily habit tracker built on one honest idea: small habits done daily lead to big success. It does not promise to transform your life in a week, sell you a coaching programme or lock your progress behind a subscription. It gives you a short daily list, a tick for each habit you complete, a streak you will not want to break, and a clear percentage showing how today went. That visible, daily honesty is what keeps people consistent long after motivation fades.

How the app works

Open the app and today's date is already showing, with your habits listed and today's progress at the top as a percentage and a progress bar. Tap a habit to mark it done; tap again to undo. Each habit carries a streak counter — the flame number that grows for every consecutive day you complete it — calculated automatically from your history, including the fair rule that a streak is not broken until the day actually ends without a tick. The app opens with five starter habits chosen for a balanced day: Namaz, Recite Holy Quran, Exercise, Learning AI and Video Idea. Through Manage Habits you can delete any of them and add your own in seconds — reading, study, walking, revision, writing, medication, anything you want to do daily.

The History panel keeps your record organised by date. Open any past day to see exactly which habits were done and which were missed, together with that day's score as a percentage, with the last thirty days kept ready to review. There are no accounts to create, no feeds, no followers and no social pressure — just you and your own record.

Who it helps

Students building a study and revision routine, especially around exams. Anyone strengthening daily worship, with Namaz and Quran recitation ready as starter habits and prayer consistency visible day by day. Teachers, workers and parents adding exercise, reading or learning to a crowded day. Creators keeping a daily idea habit. In short, anyone who has started a good habit with enthusiasm and lost it quietly within a fortnight — the streak and the daily score exist precisely for that moment.

Your data stays on your device

Your habits and your daily ticks are stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device, under keys used only by this app. Nothing is uploaded, there is no Loona server copy of your routine, and no one can see which habits you track. The app works offline once loaded and can be installed on your home screen as a PWA. The one honest caution: because the record lives in your browser, clearing browser data can erase it — consistency is backed up by habit, not by a cloud.
